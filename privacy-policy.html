Privacy Policy for QR Scanner

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Overview

QR Scanner is a QR code scanning application. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to provide and improve the app.

Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account to scan QR codes. QR codes are processed for the purpose of providing the scanning feature. If a QR code contains personal information, that information may be displayed to you as part of the scan result.

Camera Permission

The app may request camera permission because camera access is required to scan QR codes. Camera access is used for the scanning feature and is not intended to be used to identify you.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, and advertising-related information in accordance with their policies and applicable laws.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, consent, and regional requirements.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising. Third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app. However, no electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
